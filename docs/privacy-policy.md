# Nail Talk Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Nail Talk is a community app for Vietnamese nail and beauty professionals in the United States. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

## Information we collect

- Account information: username, display name, optional email, optional phone number, avatar, and profile bio.
- Community content: chat messages, marketplace posts, job posts, housing posts, saved items, and uploaded images you choose to share.
- Device/session information: a device identifier, platform, app version, and login/session data used to keep chat and account access working.
- Server diagnostics: request logs and technical metadata used for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and service reliability.

## How we use information

- To provide login, profile, chat, marketplace, job, housing, and movie preview features.
- To show community posts and messages to other members where you choose to publish or send them.
- To maintain app security, prevent spam or abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
- To improve the reliability and usability of Nail Talk.

## Payments and transactions

The current App Store review build does not include in-app purchases, subscriptions, external checkout, or paid movie unlock. Movie content shown in the app is free community preview content.

Marketplace prices, job salary ranges, and room or rental amounts are listing information provided by members. Nail Talk does not process payments or transactions for those listings.

## Sharing

Content you post in public areas, group rooms, listings, or chats may be visible to other members depending on where you share it.

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only when needed to operate the service, comply with law, protect users, or prevent abuse.

## Data retention

Account data and user content may remain stored while your account is active or as needed for service operation, safety, legal, or backup purposes. You may contact support to request account or data assistance.

## Children

Nail Talk is not intended for children under 13.
